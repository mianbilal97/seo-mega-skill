## Interpret practitioner notes carefully

| Source idea | Application |
| --- | --- |
| Topical authority formula, ranking states, initial ranking and re-ranking timelines | Use as practitioner vocabulary. Observe discovery and page/query performance by cohort. Avoid inferring hidden scores or fixed time windows. |
| Target ratios for Crawl Stats, bulk publishing cadence and case-study lifts | Set site-specific targets based on architecture, season, evidence and review capacity. Interpret case outcomes within their original sample and conditions. |
| Reverse engineering, keyword difficulty, tool traffic estimates and query weighting | Compare real user task, format and proof. Declare tool and sample limits. Avoid converting estimated traffic value into a cost to rank or treating lexical metrics as ranking weights. |
| Knowledge Panels, E-E-A-T, anchor weight and bounce rate | Improve identity, credibility, usable links and conversion paths using observable checks. Research and patents offer hypotheses. Verify any claimed platform requirement in official documentation. |
| Backlink lists, profile mentions and forum outreach | Qualify real relevance, editorial independence and audience value. Follow current search and community rules. Discard unrelated prospects and manipulative placement tactics. |

## What the research numbers mean

| Pasted claim | Verified scope and operational implication |
| --- | --- |
| 88% ChatGPT citations from web search | Ahrefs analyzed 1.4 million prompts and attributed roughly 88% of cited URLs to its observed general search retrieval category. That category can include a variety of domains and is not a statement about Google ranking positions. Use as evidence to prioritize search accessibility. [Ahrefs study](https://ahrefs.com/blog/why-chatgpt-cites-pages/) |
| 97% of Google AI Overviews cite a top-20 page | seoClarity reports at least one top-20 organic URL in 97% of AI Overviews in its 432,000-keyword analysis. Its separate 362,000-keyword sample reported 94% on that per-Overview measure and 56% on the per-citation measure. Keep the two units and samples distinct. [Impact study](https://www.seoclarity.net/research/ai-overviews-impact) · [Overlap study](https://www.seoclarity.net/research/aio-rankings-overlap) |
| 72.4% of cited blog posts had an early answer | The Search Engine Land analysis found identifiable direct-answer passages in 72.4% of sampled cited blog posts. Use short clear answers where helpful. The statistic does not establish a causal effect, a universal length or an eligibility rule. [Study](https://searchengineland.com/how-to-get-cited-by-chatgpt-the-content-traits-llms-quote-most-464868) |
| Brand mentions 0.664 vs backlinks 0.218 | These are Spearman correlations with AI Overview brand visibility in an Ahrefs brand study. They are neither ranking weights nor evidence that a mention causes a citation. Test useful earned coverage for the relevant audience. [Ahrefs study](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overview-brand-correlation/) |
| AI citations were 25.7% fresher | An Ahrefs study reported that citations across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Copilot and Gemini had lower average age since publication than the organic search results comparison group. The comparison is about average publication age across the sample and differs from its last-update and Google AI Overview findings. Prioritize substantive updates when facts change. [Ahrefs study](https://ahrefs.com/blog/do-ai-assistants-prefer-to-cite-fresh-content/) |
| EcomBalance ranked first and appeared in AI answers | Case result presented in the user-pasted strategy. No independent baseline, ranking snapshot or causal attribution included in supplied content. Treat it as reported and omit from predictions or public proof unless verified. |

Aim to verify a claim at its primary source. For each important SEO test record whether the finding comes from an official rule, direct observation, third-party descriptive study or working hypothesis. Reuse study percentages as context only.
